Settings & Links
🤝 Clients & property owners
Who owns each property, and who manages it. An owner with a login sees only their own property — their bookings, income, cleaning and inspections — and nothing else of the business.
🔑 Permissions
Who can do what. Tick to grant. Owners always keep full access, so you can't lock yourself out.
🏠 Properties
Every property bookings, cleaning and invoices can be scoped to. New properties don't yet get an outbound iCal feed automatically (see Roadmap p1-1) — everything else (Calendar, Bookings, Invoices, cleaning fee) works immediately.
📣 Share to Social
Quickly prep a post for Facebook or Instagram. Facebook opens its share dialog directly; Instagram has no official way to pre-fill a post from a website, so this copies the caption for you to paste after opening the app.
🏢 Organizations
Internal tool — spins up a brand-new customer organization plus its first owner login in one step. Not visible to customer orgs.
📡 Outbound iCal Feeds
Paste these into Airbnb, Booking.com and LekkeSlaap to automatically block dates when you add bookings in HEP.
