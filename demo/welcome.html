What it actually does

Not a booking engine and not a spreadsheet. The operational middle, which is where the day goes.

📅

Every calendar, synced

Airbnb, Booking.com and LekkeSlaap pull in over iCal, so one view shows what is actually booked and a double-booking cannot creep in.

🧹

Cleaners who know the plan

Turnovers schedule themselves off checkouts. Your team gets a phone-friendly portal to log cleans, submit inventory and mark availability — no app to install.

💬

Guest messages, on time

Pre-arrival, mid-stay and post-stay messages ready to send, with door codes and WiFi filled in per property — and kept out of the page source.

💰

Real income, not estimates

Upload a platform statement and it reads the figures — gross, commission, fees, what actually landed. Reconciled against your own bookings.

🔍

Inspections that go somewhere

A report that finds a cracked light switch raises a task, shared with the owner, tracked to done. Photos attached.

🔐

Roles you control

Tick who can see pay, edit the website or upload statements. The database enforces it, so hiding a button is not the whole story.

Your owners get their own dashboard

Income, occupancy, an availability calendar colour-coded by platform, cleaning history and inspection reports — for their property only, in their own language. English, German, Czech, Polish and Spanish.

It is the thing almost no small agency can offer, and it is the reason owners stay.
